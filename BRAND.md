# MOB - Brand guide

Revision 1 · 10 September 2026

## The idea

**MOB. Your world, within reach.**

Category: personal AI computer. One-sentence description: "MOB is a personal AI computer being built to think locally, speak naturally, and work across the devices you authorize."

The brand should feel calm, capable, precise and tactile. Let the product carry the visual interest. Explain what it can do, where the intelligence lives, and how a person stays in control. Early-stage honesty is part of the brand.

## Voice

Use short concrete sentences and specific verbs: speak, find, draft, review, stop, connect. Lead with the user's job. Explain technical terms where they change a decision. Present one clear action per outreach message.

Use "in development", "planned", "candidate", "target" and "measured" accurately. A claim becomes measured only when the test, conditions, version, and result are recorded. Do not say "all calls", "all programs", "12-hour battery", "zero latency", "private by default", "certified", "production ready", "partnered with", or "investor interest" without supporting evidence for the full claim.

## Visual system

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Wordmark | Lowercase `mob`, close letter spacing; a small neutral dot can accompany it. Text remains legible at small sizes. |
| Background | White #FFFFFF; secondary planes #F5F5F7 |
| Text | Primary #171719; secondary #6D6D75; fine lines #E3E3E8 |
| Accent | Functional links #245ACC; use color for real state, not ambient decoration |
| Typography | Web: Arial/Helvetica/system sans. Deck: Nimbus Sans. Large, decisive headlines and generous margins. |
| Scale | Web title 60-110 px; body 17-23 px; controls at least comfortably touchable. Deck 52+ px titles, 22-28 px body. |
| Images | White/silver device on a quiet background. Preserve the design, cable direction, physical controls and credible thickness. |
| Label | Every render or example must be clearly described as a concept. Never present it as prototype photography. |
| Motion | Subtle, short transitions with reduced-motion support. No distracting scroll traps or fake progress. |
| Layout | Spacious editorial sections, one focal point at a time. Avoid dashboard clutter in the consumer story. |

The supplied front, rear, and desk images are industrial design concepts. They may illustrate the prelaunch story and investor deck with clear labels. They are not dimensionally exact drawings and must not be used as working-prototype evidence or placed in a Kickstarter hardware funding campaign.

## Product story

1. Your work spans devices.
2. MOB has a computer of its own.
3. Speak an intention or use the iOS companion.
4. Review the scope and let the harness act on approved devices.
5. Inspect the result and stop or take over when needed.

Three story pillars: **The mind is inside. Your reach, your rules. Control you can feel.**

## Consistent calls to action

- Consumer prelaunch: "Get early access" / "Join the early list".
- Hardware: "Discuss a hardware partnership".
- Investor: "Read the investor pitch" / "Start a conversation".
- Campaign journey: "Follow the development".
- Actual future sales: introduce only after price, functionality, availability and terms can be substantiated.

No fake testimonials, counters, waitlist positions, discount clocks or logos implying endorsement. No registration symbol is used: trademark status has not been verified. Brand name/domain/trademark clearance remains open before commercial production.

## First-touch message pattern

"I’m Vaibhav, building MOB, a personal AI computer in development. [Specific reason the recipient fits.] [One concrete technical or investment question.] [Short, truthful stage statement.] [One 20-minute conversation request.]"

Use the same product description in the deck, site, GitHub, RFQ and email. Personalize the recipient context, not the product's facts. End with the founder's name and a simple option to decline follow-up.


## Sales message — September 2026 revision

Campaign headline: **Hand off the follow-up.** Retain **Your world, within reach.** as the broader brand line. Lead with the intended client task and the reviewable result. The initial customer hypothesis is independent technical consultants. Describe local compute, Bluetooth and Tailscale when they explain the task or answer a buyer objection. Keep unvalidated capabilities, pricing and performance clearly labelled.
