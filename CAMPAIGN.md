# MOB - Kickstarter campaign preparation

Working draft · Not a live campaign · 10 September 2026

## Campaign premise

**MOB - Your world, within reach.**

A personal AI computer designed around voice, local intelligence, and the devices you authorize.

MOB is being developed for people whose work moves between a phone, a laptop, a browser, and a remote computer. The idea is simple: say what you need, inspect the plan, and let one personal computer coordinate the next step.

## Story copy available now

Your tools are powerful. Bringing them together still takes your attention.

MOB is our proposal for a more natural way to work. A small computer with its own language model, speech, memory, and agent harness. Voice is the starting point. An iOS companion provides setup, approvals, and receipts. An enrolled device network extends where a task can happen.

We are designing the physical details with the same care as the software: a microphone switch you can feel, a protected crown, a clear status light, a magnetic back, a serviceable battery, and a USB-C lead that sits close to the body.

The goal is not invisible control. It is useful work with understandable boundaries: the target device, the action, the permission, and the result.

## What the funding page must demonstrate

This section is a production brief, not ready-to-publish product proof. Do not change the language to present tense until the integrated prototype demonstrates the capability.

1. **Local intelligence:** an uncut offline speech-to-action sequence running on MOB, with the local computer visible and the network condition explained.
2. **Authorized reach:** a real enrolled laptop receives a scoped task, prepares a useful result, and exposes a receipt. Show what happens when permission is missing.
3. **Call assistance:** a real paired-phone call, intelligible duplex audio and immediate user takeover. Label the supported phone/OS and limitations.
4. **Physical controls:** show hardware mic cutoff, crown, charging, mounting, cable deployment and battery service procedure on the actual build.
5. **Battery and thermals:** publish the full test protocol, duty cycle, cell age, temperatures, runtime and continuous-use result.

## Two-minute film treatment

| Time | Real footage to capture | Narration / purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0-15 s | Founder at a desk, real tasks spanning devices | "Your work lives across devices. Your attention connects them." |
| 15-35 s | Actual MOB prototype in hand; controls and cable | Introduce the physical computer and current development stage. |
| 35-65 s | Uncut local speech task with output and receipt | Demonstrate the core loop. Name any edits or sped-up segments explicitly. |
| 65-90 s | Enrolled laptop action and scope approval | Show where the action runs and how permission works. |
| 90-105 s | Real call assistance and takeover, if passed | Include only validated functionality; otherwise clearly exclude from rewards. |
| 105-120 s | Founder, engineering bench, manufacturing plan | State what funding completes, main risks and the specific invitation to back. |

Use real sound and the founder's voice. Do not synthesize footage of a prototype working. Photorealistic concept renders are not permitted on Kickstarter hardware/product campaigns.

## Rewards and funding goal

Candidate structure: one main device tier; a limited pilot/developer tier only if that edition can actually be delivered; optional accessories only after design and unit economics are confirmed. Avoid bundles or stretch goals that introduce new hardware before the baseline ships. No price, quantity cap, estimated shipping date, or funding goal is approved in this draft.

Set the goal from a quote-backed model: remaining development + tooling + certification + minimum batch x fully landed unit cost + packaging + fulfillment + returns/support allowance + platform/payment costs + contingency - secured funds assigned to these costs. Treat tax, duties, currency exposure, and last-mile shipping explicitly. A low public goal must not conceal an unfunded manufacturing dependency.

## Required production disclosure

- Current prototype revision, actual measured functions, unsupported functions, and known issues.
- Founder and team experience, accurately stated; no invented hardware shipping history.
- Selected manufacturing partner only after written agreement and permission to name them.
- DFM/DFT status, component sourcing and substitutes, quality/test plan, tooling and certification status.
- Shipping territories, estimates, duties/taxes policy, support and warranty terms based on real operations.
- Risks: model performance, thermal/battery fit, call routing, RF/certification, component supply, software integration, tooling and freight delays.
- Regular update commitment and what happens if scope, cost or schedule changes.

## Submission dependencies

Kickstarter's official rules require working prototypes and prohibit photorealistic renderings for hardware/product design. Current MOB assets are prelaunch/investor concepts and are not a substitute. Creator eligibility requires a qualifying supported-country identity or entity and banking setup. India is not currently a supported creator country. Establish a legitimate qualifying structure and complete verification before submission. Do not borrow an identity or use a false location.

## Checklist before taking pledges

- Integrated prototype demonstrations and published limitations.
- Battery, latency, audio, takeover and thermal results.
- Costed BOM and supplier lead times; funds cover a viable build.
- Production/quality plan and signed scope with the chosen assembler.
- Eligible creator/entity, bank and verification documents.
- Reward prices, quantity caps, shipping estimates and customer terms.
- Real prototype photography and compliant video.
- Founder review of every claim against the evidence ledger.

Sources: https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236428-what-are-the-rules-for-hardware-and-product-design-projects and https://help.kickstarter.com/en-us/articles/16236650-who-can-use-kickstarter (verified 10 September 2026).
